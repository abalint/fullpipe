# ATLAS.md — taste-blind map of native Japanese YouTube

The explore lane's supply chain. `/recommend`'s harvest edges (related-rails,
channel RSS, taste-expanded search) can only reach the neighborhood of what's
already been watched — this file is the map of what exists *outside* that
neighborhood, so "new" has a checkable meaning: **a cluster with no rated
episodes and no recent offer in the pass log.**

Rules of use (enforced in SKILL.md Step 1.5 / Step 4):

- Entries are **supply, not suggestions** — never filter or reword them through
  taste.md. Taste's jurisdiction is the exploit lane.
- `near-box` flags an entry whose *experience shape* resembles a rated vein.
  It's fine to sample, but it does **not** satisfy a novelty ask or the explore
  quota (the re-skin test).
- Risk notes are **mechanism-level** (crosstalk, low speech, TTS infestation) —
  the only kind of caution that may touch the explore lane. Gate, don't veto:
  the speech gate and the TTS blocklist decide, not the label.
- This is a living map: when a pass discovers a real cluster that isn't here,
  add it. When the ledger gains rated episodes in a cluster, it's *sampled* —
  computed from the ledger at runtime, never hard-coded here.
- Search strings are starting points; vary them freely (`〇〇してみた`,
  `〇〇 対決`, `〇〇 チャレンジ` compose with almost anything).

Tags: **fun** (natives watch it to enjoy themselves), **hybrid** (fun with a
learn/wonder hook), **edu** (edification-native). A "fun" ask draws from
fun/hybrid; a bare open pass draws from anywhere unsampled.

**Standing mechanism note — speaker count (learned 2026-08-03).** Across the first
two explore rounds, the low scores that weren't difficulty-censored were all
three-or-more-friend banter formats (★1 クレーンゲーム, ★2 陰キャ土産, ★2 芸人
ぶらり旅), while solo and duo shows doing the *same* activities landed mid or
better. That is the crosstalk mechanism, not a taste verdict on the cluster — so
it may steer the explore lane. When a cluster below offers both, take the solo or
clean-turn-taking variant; never drop the cluster.

## Comedy & performance

- **芸人の企画チャンネル** — pro comedians running games/challenges/travel on
  their own channels; TV-honed delivery, YouTube freedom.
  `芸人 チャンネル 企画` · `芸人 ドッキリ` · `芸人 検証` — fun.
  Risk: group formats = crosstalk; prefer solo/duo picks.
- **コント / スケッチ** — scripted sketch comedy (ジャルジャル-shaped); clean
  audio, repeatable lines, situational language.
  `コント 新作` · `あるあるネタ` — fun. Scripted speech = unusually mineable.
- **ネタ動画（漫才）** — full 漫才 sets posted officially.
  `漫才 ネタ 公式` — fun. Risk: speed; the record shows manzai charmed the user
  even un-understood (presenter=5, censored topic) — a known ceiling, not a no.
- **落語 / 講談** — classical solo storytelling from the 高座: one performer voicing
  every character, projected and enunciated, whole pieces posted by the performers
  themselves or by archive channels.
  `落語 名演 古典` · `<噺家名> 高座` · `講談 読み物` — hybrid. Scripted, single-voice,
  no crosstalk — among the most mineable speech on the platform. Risk: Edo-period
  register and set phrases; and the `快眠落語 / 作業用BGM` compilation tier is junk,
  not performance. Repeat the same performer deliberately — the voice is the point.
- **大喜利** — comedians improvising answers to a prompt: TV-network YouTube
  shows (テレビ朝日「まいにち大喜利」), 佐久間宣行 NOBROCK TV's answer-breakdowns,
  and the indie 大喜る人たち circuit.
  `大喜利 芸人` · `大喜利 名回答` — fun. Short, punchy, wordplay-dense turns with a
  clean turn-taking structure (MC reads, each answers). Risk: the panel shows put
  5-6 comedians in a room — laughter overlaps; the duo breakdown format
  (a host + one comedian explaining answers) is the clean variant.
  First offered 2026-09-04.

- **すべらない話系トーク** — comedians telling polished stories to camera or
  each other; single-speaker storytelling at high speech density.
  `芸人 トーク エピソード` · `すべらない話` — fun.
- **ラジオドラマ / オーディオドラマ** — scripted multi-voice fiction posted as
  full plays: modern indie audio-drama channels (5–6 声優 + SFX/BGM) and
  archived NHK/commercial radio dramas.
  `オーディオドラマ 声優 フルボイス` · `ラジオドラマ 名作` — hybrid. Scripted,
  performed, cleanly recorded — the same mineability argument as 落語, with
  contemporary conversational register instead of Edo. **Risk (measured, not
  guessed — first sample curated 2026-09-01):** the uploader's subtitles merge
  ACROSS speaker changes, so most i+1 lines arrive as 20–30s spans covering
  several characters' turns. They fail the 1.5–15s clip bar even when the
  target word is perfect — on the first sample this killed 古民家, 妖怪,
  もてなす, 浴槽 and くじ運, and held the pool to 5 cards over 38 minutes.
  Mine it for the prep doc and the popup, not the deck. Secondary: no visuals
  at all, so difficulty runs ABOVE what the coverage % suggests (68.5% measured,
  difficulty_felt 4); and the pre-1990 archive tier carries audio-fidelity
  risk — prefer modern uploads.

- **朗読（プロ声優・ナレーターの文学朗読）** — one professional voice reading a
  whole novel or short story: 青空文庫 classics (窪田等, 西村俊彦, 枡太) and
  contemporary novels read by star 声優 (梶裕貴『推し、燃ゆ』).
  `朗読 プロ声優 小説` · `<作家名> 朗読 全編` — edu/hybrid. Single clean voice, but
  it is *written* prose, not dialogue — expect difficulty above the coverage %.
  Risk: the `睡眠用 / 作業用BGM` compilation tier is junk (long, sleepy, ad-free
  padding); take single-work uploads by named readers. First offered 2026-09-03.
- **怪談語り（怪談師の一人語り）** — one performer telling a "true" ghost story
  straight to camera or a small audience: 稲川淳二 (the genre's founder, official
  怪談劇場 channel), 島田秀平, 好井まさお, 渡辺裕薫, ぁみ.
  `実話怪談 語り 一人語り` · `<怪談師名> 怪談` — fun. Solo, dense, colloquial
  storytelling register. Risk: 稲川's delivery is idiosyncratic (mumbled, breathy);
  the younger 芸人 tellers are cleaner. The `つめあわせ 睡眠用` tier is junk.
  Distinct from 心霊スポット検証 (that's a location run, this is a telling).
  First offered 2026-09-03.

## The してみた economy (challenge & experiment)

- **検証してみた** — "we actually tested it" — urban claims, products, physics.
  `検証してみた` · `実際にやってみた` — fun.
- **ドッキリ** — prank/hidden-camera on friends/members; reaction-driven.
  `ドッキリ 仕掛けてみた` — fun. Risk: reaction-scream crosstalk.
- **大食い / デカ盛り** — competitive/spectacle eating, often with shop owner
  banter. `大食い チャレンジ` · `デカ盛り 完食` — fun. Risk: speech density
  varies (talk-while-eating vs silent grinding); gate hard.
- **激辛チャレンジ** — `激辛 チャレンジ` — fun.
- **縛り生活 / ○○だけ生活** — constraint-living for a week/month (100円だけ,
  自販機だけ, 無買い物). `1週間 生活 チャレンジ` · `0円生活` — fun.
  near-box: adjacent to the quirky-experiment vein (校庭/トド/カバン) — counts
  as explore only if the energy is entertainment, not doc.

## Food & drink entertainment

- **料理エンタメ（ガチ料理人が喋り倒す）** — リュウジ-shaped: a real cook
  talking (and drinking) straight to camera; single clear voice, useful output.
  `料理研究家 レシピ 簡単` · `晩酌 レシピ` — fun.
- **魚捌き** — きまぐれクック-shaped: buy weird/huge fish, break it down,
  cook it, talking throughout. Enormous native vein.
  `魚 捌いてみた` · `巨大魚 調理` — fun.
- **せんべろ / はしご酒** — cheap-drunk izakaya crawls, host chatting with
  camera and shopkeepers. `せんべろ はしご酒` · `大衆酒場 飲み歩き` — fun.
  Risk: izakaya noise floor.
- **再現レシピ** — recreating anime/movie/game food for real.
  `再現レシピ アニメ` — fun.
- **チェーン店・コンビニ実食レビュー** — talk-to-camera taste tests, rankings.
  `チェーン店 実食 レビュー` · `コンビニ新商品 食べてみた` — fun.
- **屋台 / 祭り / 夜市グルメ** — `屋台 食べ歩き 祭り` — hybrid.
  Risk: ambient-heavy, crosstalk in crowds; near-box: 食べ歩き rated ★2 for
  crosstalk once — pick narrated-solo variants.

## Games, quizzes, brains

- **クイズ / 謎解き対決** — QuizKnock-shaped: smart people competing at speed;
  the bridge cluster between the user's edification record and entertainment.
  `クイズ 対決 東大` · `謎解き 対決` — hybrid. Risk: some crosstalk at buzzer
  moments; speech density excellent.
- **ゲーム実況（ソロ）** — one person playing + narrating; continuous natural
  speech, emotion vocabulary. Horror 実況 = maximum reaction language.
  `ゲーム実況 ホラー` · `ゲーム実況 名作` — fun. Solo only; group = crosstalk.
- **将棋・囲碁の対局解説** — a professional narrating a real game move by move:
  棋士's own channels (中村太地, 山口恵梨子), 大盤解説会 archives, 囲碁将棋TV.
  `将棋 解説 対局` · `大盤解説 名人戦` · `囲碁 解説` — hybrid. One or two clear
  speakers over a static board, continuous explanation, a lot of "why" language;
  a whole native subculture the ledger has never touched. Risk: dense jargon
  (定跡・手筋 names) pulls the coverage estimate down; the multi-hour 大盤解説
  livestream archives (4-11 h) are the junk tier here — take the edited
  30-50 min uploads. First offered 2026-09-04.

- **マイクラ / 建築・企画ゲーム** — `マイクラ 建築 企画` — fun.
- **人狼 / ボードゲーム実況** — `人狼 実況` — fun. Risk: crosstalk high by
  design; sample only clean-turn formats.
- **科学実験エンタメ** — big/flashy experiments performed for wonder.
  `科学実験 やってみた` · `実験 巨大` — hybrid.

## Money, stuff, spectacle

- **開封 / 福袋 / クレーンゲーム** — `福袋 開封` · `クレーンゲーム 大量` — fun.
  Junk-food tier but speech-dense and easy.
- **鑑定 / 質屋 / 骨董** — treasure-hunt hook: what's it worth?
  `質屋 鑑定` · `骨董市 掘り出し物` — hybrid.
- **リサイクルショップ / せどり巡り** — `リサイクルショップ 掘り出し物` — hybrid.
  Risk: the 古道具・暮らし vlog tier is near-silent (BGM + text overlay) and passes
  the caption gate while carrying almost no speech — check density, not just the
  gate verdict; prefer the running-commentary store tours.
- **変身企画 / 美容師のビフォーアフター** — a stylist takes a volunteer and
  rebuilds their look on camera, talking to them throughout; the reveal is the
  payoff. `美容師 ビフォーアフター 変身企画` · `大変身 密着` — fun.
  Risk: speech density is middling (long silent cutting/styling stretches under
  BGM) — check tokens-per-minute, not just the gate; the tiny salon-promo tier
  is near-silent. First offered 2026-09-04.

- **高級 vs 激安 比較** — `高級 激安 比較 検証` — fun.

## Talk & people

- **街頭インタビュー** — street-corner vox-pop on money/love/work; many
  speakers, short turns, real colloquial Japanese.
  `街頭インタビュー 本音` · `街頭インタビュー 給料` — hybrid.
- **街録・人生深掘りインタビュー** — long-form life-story interviews of
  strangers. `人生 インタビュー 街録` — hybrid.
  near-box: this is the singular-person-doc shape in interview clothes.
- **切り抜き討論 / 持論トーク** — debate/opinion clip culture.
  `切り抜き 議論` — hybrid. Risk: low-effort clickbait tier; choose carefully.
- **ラジオ風二人雑談** — two knowledgeable friends riffing (ゆるラジオ family).
  `ラジオ 雑談 二人` — hybrid. near-box: ゆる哲学ラジオ ★4 already on record.
- **公開講座・講演（大学／TEDx）** — one expert speaking to a live audience for
  40-60 minutes: UTokyo Channel's 公開講座 series, 東洋文化研究所, TEDxTalks の
  日本語登壇.  `大学 公開講座 講義` · `TEDx 日本語 講演` — edu. Single projected
  voice, prepared structure, slides to scaffold the listening; unusually dense
  and unusually clean. Risk: academic register and long subordinate clauses put
  real difficulty above the coverage %; TEDx talks are short (10-20 min) while
  the university lectures run 40-75. First offered 2026-09-04.

- **Vtuber雑談・切り抜き** — real human voices (not TTS) behind avatars;
  clear solo talk. `Vtuber 雑談 切り抜き` — fun.
  Risk: heavy net-slang register; NOT the synthetic-TTS veto (real voice), but
  verify by ear if unsure.
