# taste.md — immersion taste digest

*Legible, editable digest of what the ratings actually show (52 rated episodes as
of 2026-07-13). This is what /recommend ranks against. Edit freely — correct me
where I've over-read the data.*

## Reliably rate high (★4–5)

- **Immersive 密着 documentaries that follow one real, unusual person.** The
  spine of the taste. ★5 森でひとりサバイバルする15歳, ★5 しごとリアル
  (芸人が本気で現場バイト), ★4 洋傘職人89歳 ("fascinating"). A warm off-camera
  interviewer + a genuinely singular subject = a lock. Both `follow=more`
  channels live here (1日見てもいいですか?, しごとリアル).
- **Atmospheric walking / exploration with a sense of place.** ★5 閉鎖遊園地
  ガマランドを歩く 4K, ★5 銀座散歩. Place carries mood; not just B-roll.
- **廃村・秘境 exploration with a story in it.** ★4 樫山集落 廃村探訪, ★4 九州
  秘境5000キロ旅 ("fascinating"). The ruins need a human/historical hook —
  the ones that are just "look, an abandoned thing" land lower (see below).
- **Quirky one-off experiments / stunts.** ★4 3年放置の校庭を車でならす, ★4
  トドにデッドボール, ★4 東大物理女子の80万円カバンの中身. "loved_format."
- **Geography / topography explainers.** ★4 東京はなぜ坂が多いか（地形解説）,
  ★4 高島平団地の暗渠散歩 — the "why is this place shaped like this" angle.
- **Free-spirit solo travel / 車中泊.** ★4 雨のハイエース車中泊, ★4 九州秘境旅.
- **Rakugo & mystery/philosophy radio.** ★4 落語 春風亭一之輔「鈴ヶ森」, ★4
  ハーメルンの笛吹き男の真相 (ゆる哲学ラジオ, "fascinating").

## Bail on (★1–2)

- **Low-stakes talking-head 雑談 vlogs.** Mom/daily-life chatter with no stakes:
  ★2 ミキティのママトーク, ★2 一般女の日常, ★2 アラサー2人の雑談室, ★2 くまみき
  お悩み相談. Repeatedly "didnt_grab." The single clearest negative signal.
- **Rail / 乗り鉄 travel.** ★2 宗谷本線 秘境駅, ★2 富山の路面電車, ★2 太平洋
  フェリー40時間. Tagged "format_miss" more than once — trains just don't land.
- **Insects / entomology.** ★1 虫プロの昆虫観察, low biology podcast. A dead vein.
- **How-to tutorials.** ★1 星空撮影の基本 ("format_miss"). Wants stories, not lessons.
- **Food-walking duos with fast overlapping banter.** ★2 谷中銀座食べ歩き —
  the high-energy crosstalk format grates even when the topic is fine.

## Ambiguous / revisit-when-stronger

- **Traditional-craft (職人) documentaries** land all over the map: ★4 洋傘職人
  but ★3 畳/津軽塗/加賀象嵌 and ★2 楽焼. The *person and story* decide it, not
  the craft — a bare process-doc under-performs. Worth surfacing selectively.
- **Difficulty is a real ceiling, not a taste-negative.** Several ★4–5 are flagged
  `over_my_head` (銀座散歩, 高島平, Nanasuke essays): loved the presenter/place,
  understood too little to score the topic. Read these as "keep the vein, it was
  just hard" — not as taste evidence about the subject. Dense scripted 書き言葉
  narration (Nanasuke) is the recurring hard case.

## Presenter fingerprint (rolled up)

Warm and characterful, two flavors both welcome: (a) a singular *real subject*
captured by an enthusiastic, reaction-heavy off-camera interviewer (密着 style),
or (b) calm, evocative documentary narration over strong footage. Dislikes:
low-energy solo talking-head with no stakes, and fast overlapping variety-show
crosstalk. Synthetic-TTS narration (ゆっくり/VOICEROID/ずんだもん) is a hard no —
can't listen to it.
