# taste.md — immersion taste digest

*What the ratings show, at the confidence they actually support (241 rated episodes
as of 2026-09-29; 62 of them added since the previous digest of 2026-09-17, plus the
first four whole-series verdicts). This is what /recommend ranks against. Edit freely.*

## Read this first: what the instrument can and can't tell us

Each episode has **one** number, recorded **once**, minutes after watching. That
measurement carries mood, time of day, fatigue, and difficulty along with taste,
and nothing re-measures it later. 241 episodes split across three dozen genres is
still two to ten episodes per vein — enough to form a hypothesis, and in two or
three places now enough to call a lead, not enough to establish a rule.

Two consequences the ranking pass should respect:

- **What the stars mean, in the user's words (2026-09-29):** ★3 = *ok*. ★2 = *I
  didn't really like it*. ★1 = *I hate this, and probably didn't finish it*.
  So:
  - **★3 is "ok", not a negative and not a draw.** Watchable, fine to serve again
    as filler, but nothing pulls toward more of it. 103 of 241 episodes sit there.
    A ★3 cluster says the cluster is *acceptable*, not that it's a vein, and must
    not be written up as a demotion. The explore lane produces ★3 walls by design.
  - **★2 is a real dislike** (52 episodes). Not a hate, not a veto on the
    cluster, but a genuine thumbs-down on that episode.
  - **★1 is the strongest negative the record has, and usually an abandoned
    watch.** Ten episodes: 裁判傍聴記 (AI images), まぐろ船 (no speech), パチンコ,
    OVER THE SUN, 変なホテル, クレーンゲーム, 高岡早紀 cooking (audio 1), 統一教会
    座談会 (channel blocked), 星空撮影 how-to, 虫プロ 昆虫観察. Every one with axes
    tapped has topic_pull 1 or presenter ≤ 2. Treat the *specific shape* of a ★1 as
    a near-veto in the exploit lane.
- **Stated reasons outrank inferred patterns.** When a note says *why* ("all AI
  images made it unwatchable"), that's evidence about a mechanism and is usable at
  n=1. A bare star cluster with no stated reason is a correlation across a
  handful of episodes, and is not. The two are kept in separate sections below.
- **The first episode of anything new is rated high; the score settles after.**
  Stated by the user 2026-09-29 ("I tend to rate high new video types, then
  settle into a normalized score after getting used to them") and plain in the
  record: channels with three or more ratings average **★4.2 on their first
  episode, ★3.5 on the second, ★3.4 thereafter**; formats go ★3.5 → ★3.0. The
  cleanest cases: 窪田等 ★5 → 3, 3, 3; しごとリアル ★5 → 2, 2, 2; 1日見て ★5, 4 → mostly
  ★3; Koto☆Hana's ★5s cluster early. So:
  - a single ★4–5 on a new format or channel is *novelty plus maybe taste* — a
    lead to sample again, not a vein;
  - a slide from ★5 to ★3–4 over later episodes is the score *settling*, not the
    vein weakening — don't write it up as a decline;
  - what counts is the **settled level** (third episode on). A channel that is
    still at ★4 after the novelty is gone — GOLDNRUSH (4, 4, 4, 4), ななすけ
    (3 → 4 → 4), Koto☆Hana (still ★4 on episodes 8–12) — is the strongest signal
    this record can give.

Across 66 episodes no presenter ≤ 2 has ever scored above ★3, and across 63 no
presenter ≥ 4 has scored below ★3. That held through 62 new ratings, which makes
it the only rule in this file.

`topic_pull` is the refinement this round adds. In the middle it still leaks both
ways — seventeen topic_pull=4 episodes landed at ★3 (e.g. 指詰め, 鑑定, 歩荷, ゴミ屋敷,
PIVOT 自衛隊, 朝ポキ 中国情勢, サイエントーク イグノーベル), and eleven topic_pull=3
episodes landed at ★4 (福のラジオ, 推し燃ゆ, 宇多田ヒカル GOLDNRUSH, Rebuild, 加乃子).
But the **extremes are floors**: all 13 topic_pull=1 episodes scored ★1–2, and the
2026-09-17 expertise block is where most of them came from (パチンコ, 車レビュー,
サッカー戦術, 和歌山カレー事件, ガチテニス) — each also presenter 2.

Practical reading for the ranking pass: **rank on the speaker before the subject,
but don't expect a good voice to rescue a subject the user has no stake in.** A
candidate from a channel whose presenter profile matches the fingerprint below is
a better bet than an on-topic candidate from an unknown voice; a candidate whose
subject is pure sports/cars/gambling/industry analysis is a poor exploit bet
regardless of who's talking.

## Stated reasons — trust these even at n=1

These come from the user's own notes, from explicit asks logged in /recommend, or
from axes that are near-tautological.

- **AI-generated imagery is disqualifying.** ★1 裁判傍聴記, note verbatim: "All AI
  images made it unwatchable. Interesting topic though." A good topic does not
  survive it. Same family as the synthetic-TTS veto.
- **Synthetic-TTS narration (ゆっくり/VOICEROID/ずんだもん) is a hard no** — stated
  directly, outside the ratings.
- **Grating sound design sinks an otherwise-right audio drama.** ★3 happyhillmusic
  『Wings〜由紀恵の盗聴記録』, note: "In theory this would be great, but there is a
  bubbling sound effect in many of the scenes that is driving me crazy." In audio
  formats the mix *is* the presentation — a repetitive or intrusive effect bed is
  the audio-drama equivalent of AI imagery.
- **Rain atmosphere is a draw; camping gear isn't.** ★3 横山三等兵 大雨ソロキャンプ,
  note: "I liked the rainy day stuff, but the camping is whatever." Pairs with ★4
  雨の車中泊 from the earlier record. Weather-as-mood is the ingredient; gear
  reviews and set-up footage are not.
- **Low speech density kills a pick regardless of subject.** ★1 まぐろ船 ("Very low
  speech density"), ★2 東京発酵スポット ("Low language density"). Nothing to hear =
  nothing to mine. The speech gate bites at selection time now.
- **Audio-only is wanted, in quantity.** 2026-09-03: "I've been enjoying the audio
  dramas." 2026-09-05: "I need about 40 hours of content, I want 20 hours of it to
  be audio based. audio dramas, podcasts, radio shows." September's ratings back
  it: the podcast/radio formats now hold fifteen ★4s. Treat audio-native formats
  as a standing half of the diet, not a novelty lane.
- **Trivia / quiz shows were asked for** (2026-09-01) and **manga read aloud with
  discussion** (2026-09-01) and **GTA6 talk** (2026-08-23). Requested topics; the
  game-talk request has now paid off (see podcasts below).
- **Bad audio caps the score — now a firm rule.** `audio_fidelity` ≤ 2 → mean ★2.3
  over 33 episodes, never above ★3. This round's additions are mostly archival:
  two old 落語 recordings (志ん朝 明烏, 歌丸 お茶汲み), 深澤弘's last 野球実況, the NHK
  子ども科学電話相談 clip, outdoor handheld 密着. Old broadcast archives are a
  known trap: great material, capped by the recording.
- **Difficulty is a ceiling, not a taste-negative.** Episodes at difficulty=5 with
  the comprehension axes censored still average about the record. ★5 荒木町散歩 and
  ★4 裏渋谷散歩 (both ななすけ, both topic-censored), ★4 サンドウィッチマン 漫才, and
  the quiz family. Keep those veins; they were hard.

## Mechanism notes (inferred, but consistent across many episodes)

- **It is turn discipline, not speaker count.** Audio dramas with five or six
  voices are the best-rated thing in the record; *duos* riffing unscripted rate
  ★2 (かまいたち, バカリズム&佐久間, ラランド×又吉, 水溜りボンド, 今田×千原). What the
  lows share is improvised, overlapping chat with nobody steering; what the highs
  share is a script, a host, or a performer holding the floor. Prefer *hosted or
  performed* over *riffing*, at any headcount.
- **Q&A / お悩み相談 / low-stakes 雑談** (★2 佐野, ★2 同棲カップル, ★2 くまみき, ★2
  ミキティ, ★2 一般女, ★2 アラサー2人, ★2 Yu in London, ★1 OVER THE SUN (new), ★2
  神田伯山 radio (new)). n=10, nothing above ★3, every tapped one presenter ≤ 2.
  Counterexample stands: ★4 にんじんママ is the same format with a concrete problem.
- **presenter=1 is a floor.** Eight episodes, all ★1–2.
- **Old recordings cap archival gold.** 落語 masters, 野球実況, NHK radio clips —
  audio 2 every time, ★3 every time. If an archival candidate has a remastered or
  studio-quality upload, take that one.

## Presenter fingerprint (rolled up)

Three flavors, all confirmed again this round:

- (a) **A performed voice** — professional voice actors in an ensemble, a comedian
  delivering material, a 落語家 on the 高座 (in a clean recording), a single
  narrator reading prose (when the text suits). Clean, projected, enunciated, with
  a script or a routine behind it.
- (b) **Calm, evocative narration or a curious walker over strong footage** —
  地形 walks, island travel, rain and long hauls. Solo, unhurried, driven by a
  question, a place or the weather.
- (c) **A host running a conversation with one guest who makes things** — music
  and creator podcasts (GOLDNRUSH), game-dev talk, 声優 web radio, radio
  interviews. Two people talking *to each other* about craft, under someone's
  steering.

Dislikes: flat or sneering delivery (presenter 1 is a floor), fan-facing Q&A,
low-stakes 雑談, improvised riffing without a host, solo experts to camera on
subjects with no pull (sports tactics, cars, gambling, industry analysis),
grating effect beds in audio drama, archival recordings too muddy to hear, and
breathy theatrical solo telling (怪談, tentative). Hard nos: synthetic-TTS
narration and AI-generated imagery.
